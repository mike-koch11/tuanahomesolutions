Investment Property Renovation in Dallas–Fort Worth

Tuana Home Solutions works with Dallas–Fort Worth real estate investors on fix-and-flip and rental property renovations. The starting point is the property condition, the intended buyer or tenant and a clear description of the work.

Match the scope to the investment plan

A rental refresh and a resale renovation can require different choices. Identify essential repairs, visible improvements and the level of finish appropriate to the property before committing to a construction budget.

Renovation work for investment properties

A scope may combine kitchen and bathroom updates, flooring, painting, tile, carpentry and other agreed improvements. We help organize these items into a project-specific estimate.

Make estimates easier to compare

Provide a room-by-room work list and material assumptions. Clarify included labor, supplied materials, exclusions and how changes will be documented. Comparable scopes make contractor estimates more useful.

Plan for the actual property condition

Access, material availability, conditions discovered during demolition and required approvals can affect the schedule and scope. Discuss these factors early and document agreed changes during the project.

Questions About Your Project

Do you guarantee a resale value or investment return?

No. Construction scope and pricing can be defined, but resale value, rent and investment returns depend on market conditions and other factors beyond the renovation.

Can you renovate a property before listing it?

Yes. Share the property location, planned improvements and desired listing timeline so the scope and schedule can be evaluated.

What should an investor provide for the first discussion?

Provide the property city, photos, access arrangements, a repair or improvement list, intended use and preferred timing.
