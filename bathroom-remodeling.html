Bathroom Remodeling in Dallas–Fort Worth

Tuana Home Solutions renovates bathrooms for Dallas–Fort Worth homeowners who want a more practical layout, updated finishes or a different shower and vanity arrangement. We start with the space you have and the changes you want.

A bathroom planned for everyday use

Think about shower access, storage, lighting, cleaning and the number of people using the room. Those decisions help define the renovation before selecting finishes.

Bathroom remodeling services

Depending on your project, the scope may include demolition, shower renovation, tile, vanities, countertops, flooring, paint and finish carpentry. Plumbing and electrical changes need project-specific assessment and appropriate trade coordination.

What affects the cost?

Room size, tile selection, shower configuration, glass, fixture choices, utility changes and conditions found during demolition affect the total. A surface refresh and a full shower rebuild should be estimated as different scopes.

Prepare for the project

Share photos and note any leaks, damaged surfaces or recurring issues. Discuss access, material selections and use of another bathroom during construction. The estimate should distinguish included work from additional work discovered later.

Questions About Your Project

Can you replace a tub with a shower?

A tub-to-shower conversion can be considered after reviewing the room, drainage, layout and proposed shower design. An on-site assessment is needed to define the work.

Will moving plumbing affect the estimate?

Moving fixtures can change the plumbing work, demolition and restoration required. Those changes should be identified in the project scope.

Can you give a fixed price from one photo?

Photos help with the initial discussion, but room measurements, scope and existing conditions are needed for a project-specific estimate.
