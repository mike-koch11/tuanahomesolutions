Kitchen Remodeling in Dallas–Fort Worth

A kitchen renovation should make everyday cooking, storage and gathering easier. Tuana Home Solutions helps Dallas–Fort Worth homeowners plan kitchen updates around the room, the work needed and their priorities.

Plan the work around how you use your kitchen

Start with what needs to change: limited storage, worn surfaces, an awkward layout or a kitchen that no longer fits the rest of your home. We discuss those priorities before defining the renovation scope.

Kitchen remodeling services

Your project may include cabinetry, countertops, backsplash tile, flooring, painting, trim and layout changes. The written estimate identifies which work and materials are included. Structural changes and specialist trade work need to be evaluated for the particular property.

What affects the cost?

Cabinet choices, countertop materials, room size, demolition, layout changes and existing conditions all affect the estimate. Keeping the current layout and changing utility locations are different scopes; compare estimates using the same work items and material assumptions.

Planning your renovation

Share photos, your city, the changes you want and your preferred timing. An on-site assessment helps identify the scope, material decisions and access needs. Review the written estimate and agreed schedule before work begins.

Questions About Your Project

Can I remodel without changing the kitchen layout?

Yes. Depending on the condition of the room, a renovation can focus on cabinets, countertops, backsplash, flooring and finishes while keeping the existing layout.

How long does a kitchen remodel take?

Timing depends on scope, material availability, existing conditions and any required approvals. Your project schedule should be based on the agreed work and material lead times.

What should I send when requesting an estimate?

Include your city, photos, a description of the changes, your preferred timing and any material selections you have already made.
