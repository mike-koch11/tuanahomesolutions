Full Home Remodeling in Dallas–Fort Worth

A full home renovation connects decisions across several rooms. Tuana Home Solutions works with Dallas–Fort Worth homeowners and property investors to define the improvements, organize the work and select finishes that work together.

Define the renovation before demolition

List the rooms, repairs and finish changes you want. Separate essential repairs from optional improvements so the estimate reflects your priorities and the condition of the property.

Full home remodeling services

A project may combine kitchen and bathroom remodeling, flooring, tile, interior painting, carpentry, closets and selected layout changes. Each item belongs in the written scope so everyone understands what the project includes.

A sequence that fits the property

Demolition, structural or specialist trade work, installation and finishing need a coordinated sequence. Material lead times, access to the home and required approvals can affect that sequence.

What affects the cost?

The number of rooms, existing conditions, material selections, utility or structural changes and the amount of finish work affect the estimate. Compare complete scopes instead of comparing only the total price.

Questions About Your Project

Do you work on investment properties?

Yes. Tuana Home Solutions works with homeowners and real estate investors on renovation projects, including properties being prepared for resale or rental.

Can I stay in the house during a remodel?

That depends on the work, safe access and whether essential rooms and utilities remain usable. Discuss occupancy and staging before agreeing to a construction schedule.

Can layout changes be included?

Layout changes can be evaluated as part of the scope. Structural conditions and required approvals must be reviewed before defining that work.
